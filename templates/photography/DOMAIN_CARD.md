# photography — Domain Card

## Lens
Photographic genre as a craft discipline — light, lens, the held
moment. The subject category is incidental; photographic intent leads.

## Templates
| id | form factor | grader | use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| photography_editorial_portrait | photo 4:5 1024x1280 | text_fidelity_sparse | magazine portrait of a single subject; deliberate lighting and lens choice |
| photography_cinematic_still | photo 16:9 1920x1088 | text_fidelity_sparse | film-frame still; anamorphic look, narrative beat, mood-board pitches |
| photography_documentary_scene | photo 3:2 1536x1024 | text_fidelity_sparse | reportage frame in a real-world setting; available light, candid posture |

## Choose photography over its siblings when
- The brief is about **photographic craft** — lighting, lens, grain,
  film stock, available light. Pick photography.
- A subject category dominates the brief instead. Send fashion lookbooks
  to `fashion`, food menus to `food`, advertising hero shots to
  `advertising`.
- The output is a designed graphic with overlaid copy. That is not
  photography — try `social_media`, `advertising`, or a poster template.

## Bilingual notes
All three templates render zh-CN + en. Captions stay sparse: photography
images are graded against `text_fidelity_sparse` because text is
incidental, never the focus.

## Forbidden
Real public figures, named celebrities, real news brand overlays,
HDR-glossy plastic finish, copyrighted franchise references.
