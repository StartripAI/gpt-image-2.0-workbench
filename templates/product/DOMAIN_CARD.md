# product — domain card

**This domain is FOR**
- Branded packaging concept renderings (boxes, bottles, pouches) on minimal studio backdrops
- Single-product feature-callout boards for launch microsites and design press
- Multi-variant comparison boards that aid product-line decisions

**This domain is NOT for**
- Ecommerce sales tiles, marketplace cards, or category banners (use `ecommerce`)
- Industrial machinery, cutaways, or factory-floor signage (use `industrial`)
- Food packaging specifically (use `food` when authored)
- Lifestyle / model-in-use photography or retail-shelf context

**Key visual axes (what differentiates the three templates here)**
1. **Subject density** — one package alone (packaging concept) vs one product with three callouts (feature) vs three variants side-by-side (comparison)
2. **Aspect & viewing context** — 1:1 design-press portrait vs 16:9 microsite hero vs 4:5 decision-support board
3. **Annotation register** — pure brand-mood (none) vs leader-line callouts (some) vs structured row-by-row checkboxes (full table)

**Templates in this domain**
- `product_packaging_concept` — 1:1 packaging hero (box/bottle/pouch) on studio backdrop
- `product_feature_callout` — 16:9 product hero with three leader-line feature callouts
- `product_comparison_board` — 4:5 side-by-side three-variant comparison with feature checkboxes
