# kids_illustration — Domain Card

## Lens
Children's-book and early-learning illustration — friendly cast,
rounded shapes, primary palette, large legible type. The lineage is
Eric Carle, Oliver Jeffers, Beatrice Alemagna, Jon Klassen, Hervé
Tullet — never anime, never academic, never photoreal.

## FOR
- Two-page picture-book spreads with friendly cast and dialogue.
- Preschool workbook pages — counting, matching, coloring activities.
- Bedtime / early-reader narrative scenes for ages 3-7.

## NOT FOR
- Anime character sheets or comic panels — use `anime/`.
- Educational classroom diagrams or biology figures — use
  `academic/`.
- Sequential color-comic panels for older readers — use
  `comic_book/`.
- Photoreal stock-photo of children — use `photography/`.

## Key axes
- Rounded shapes, simple silhouettes; never sharp anatomical realism.
- Primary-plus-pastel palette; one warm accent on a soft paper ground.
- Large rounded sans type set generously; never tiny editorial type.
- Friendly subject matter only — no horror, no violence, no
  sexualization.

## Templates in domain
- `kids_illustration_storybook_spread` — poster, 16:9 (1920x1088),
  `text_fidelity_dense`. Two-page spread with narration band and a
  dialogue line.
- `kids_illustration_workbook_page` — infographic, 4:5 (1024x1280),
  `text_fidelity_dense`. Preschool counting / matching / coloring
  activity page with title, instruction, and item count label.

## Forbidden
Anime large eyes, photoreal rendering, academic diagram register,
horror or violence, sexualized characters, real cartoon-IP characters,
copyrighted franchise marks.
