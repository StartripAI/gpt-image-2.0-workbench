# Domain: comic_book

American / Western comic-book aesthetic — bold inks, halftone, US-comic
letterforms. Variant covers and multi-panel interior pages.

## FOR
- Fictional-IP variant covers with masthead + issue number
- 4-6 panel interior pages with speech balloons and panel captions
- Bold inked outlines, four-color halftone, Ben-Day dot midtones
- Sequential storytelling at a single-page granularity

## NOT FOR
- Manga / anime screen-tone or cel-shading (use `anime/`)
- Storyboard pencils for advertising (use `advertising/`)
- Painterly watercolor sequential art (use `watercolor_illustration/`)
- 3D-CG comic renders (use `gaming/` for stylized CG)
- Children's storybook softness (use `kids_illustration/`)

## Key visual axes
- Inked outline weight + brush-weighted line variation
- Four-color halftone shading and Ben-Day dot midtones
- Speech balloon discipline: crisp borders, clear tail direction
- US-comic letterform — upper-case, never manga lettering
- Fictional-IP-only — no Marvel / DC trade dress, characters, or logos

## Templates in domain
- `comic_book_variant_cover` — poster, 2:3, single hero variant cover
- `comic_book_panel_page` — comic_page, 2:3, 4-6 panel interior page
