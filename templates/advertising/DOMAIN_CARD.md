# Domain: advertising

Premium production-grade brand creative — the work an agency would
present to a client before media buy.

## FOR
- Brand campaign hero key visuals (OOH-grade single-image concepts)
- Photoreal billboard placement mockups for media-buy review
- Three-panel storyboards for 30-second commercial pitches
- Conceptual ad-creative exploration with disciplined typography

## NOT FOR
- Social-media UGC posts or feed-native short-form content
  (use `social_media/`)
- In-game UI overlays or fictional HUDs (use `gaming/`)
- Product packaging or retail SKU mockups (use `product/`)
- Web or app UI mockups (use `uiux/`)

## Key visual axes
- Conceptual boldness vs. literal product shot
- Production polish (cinematic lighting, restrained palette)
- OOH-safe typography hierarchy (tagline, product, CTA)
- Storyboard continuity (character + prop persistence across cuts)

## Templates in domain
- `advertising_campaign_key_visual` — poster, 16:9, brand hero KV
- `advertising_billboard_mockup` — mockup, 16:9, photoreal placement
- `advertising_storyboard_3frame` — comic_page, 3:2, 30-second TVC arc
