# infographic_data — Domain Card

## Lens
Information-design / dataviz output — bars, lines, donuts and
small-multiples with annotated insight, in the lineage of FT Visual
& Data, Reuters Graphics, and Information is Beautiful. The chart
is the artifact; the headline is its caption.

## FOR
- Editorial dataviz dashboards with four panels of charts plus
  insight callouts.
- Single-chart explainer figures with annotated pull-outs and a
  source line.
- Print-style explainers for newsletters, policy briefs, and
  long-form editorial.

## NOT FOR
- Artifact-shaped business cards (SWOT quadrants, pitch slides) —
  use `business/`.
- App-frame UI dashboards or web admin screens — use `uiux/`.
- Decorative hand-drawn data illustration without numerical accuracy
  — use `watercolor_illustration/` for that mood.
- Academic figures with citations and equations — use `academic/`.

## Key axes
- Numerical accuracy and tick legibility beat decoration.
- Restrained accent-hue palette on a neutral paper ground.
- Print-style register, never an app-window mockup.
- Always fictional sources — no real news brands, no real datasets.

## Templates in domain
- `infographic_data_dashboard` — infographic, 16:9 (1920x1088),
  `text_fidelity_dense`. Four-panel editorial explainer with KPI
  tiles, insight callout, and headline.
- `infographic_data_chart_explainer` — infographic, 4:5 (1024x1280),
  `text_fidelity_dense`. Single hero chart with three annotated
  callouts and a source line.

## Forbidden
3D extruded charts, rainbow ramps, app-frame chrome, glossy plastic
gradients, photographic stock, real news-brand logos, real public
figures.
