# automotive — Domain Card

## Lens
Vehicle-as-protagonist work — cars, motorcycles, EVs, and motorsport
artifacts where the machine itself carries the frame. The badge,
silhouette, and stance are the subject; figures, if present, are
incidental.

## FOR
- Cinematic single-vehicle hero ads (campaign key visuals)
- Point-of-sale dealership posters with price and financing
- Motorsport announcement plates and showroom retail prints

## NOT FOR
- Fictional spacecraft or speculative concept vehicles
  (use `science_fiction_concept`)
- Factory-floor machines, robotic arms, conveyor lines
  (use `industrial`)
- Generic brand campaigns where the vehicle is incidental
  (use `advertising`)
- Pure car photography with no commercial framing — fall back to
  `photography_cinematic_still` instead

## Key visual axes
- Vehicle silhouette and stance vs. lifestyle context
- Showroom honesty vs. cinematic dramatization
- Single-machine isolation vs. crowded automotive scene

Real automaker logos, real model badges, real-celebrity drivers,
real-dealership chains, real-circuit branding.
