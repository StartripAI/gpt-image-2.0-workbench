# food — Domain Card

## Lens
Culinary editorial — appetite, food styling, ingredient story. The
dish and the place that serves it lead.

## Templates
| id | form factor | grader | use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| food_menu_poster | poster 9:16 1088x1920 | text_fidelity_dense | restaurant wall menu poster; categorized dish list with prices |
| food_packaging_label | poster 3:4 1152x1536 | text_fidelity_dense | packaged-goods label mock; coffee bag, bottle, jar wrapper |
| food_recipe_card | infographic 4:5 1024x1280 | text_fidelity_dense | single-recipe hero card; prep/serves chips, key ingredient |

## Choose food over its siblings when
- The brief is about **the dish or the eater** — menu, recipe, label
  story, ingredient. Pick food.
- The brief is a paid food-service ad campaign. Send to `advertising`.
- A generic packaged-product carton outside food. Send to `product`.
- The brief is documentary food photography (a market scene, a
  kitchen at work). Use `photography_documentary_scene`.

## Bilingual notes
All three render zh-CN + en. Menu posters, packaging labels, and
recipe cards lean heavily on typography — graders expect dense,
legible text rendering.

## Forbidden
Real famous restaurant or food-brand names, copyrighted franchise
marks, photographic dish images on the menu poster, HDR oversaturation,
real-celebrity endorsements.
