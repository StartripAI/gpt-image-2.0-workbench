# typography — Domain Card

## FOR
Briefs where **type is the subject of the artwork** — specimen
sheets, lettering pieces, calligraphy posters, foundry plates.
The letterforms are the picture, not a label on top of one.

## NOT FOR
- Generic posters where text is incidental — those belong in
  `advertising` (key visuals), `business` (slides) or `events`
  (announcements).
- Magazine spreads where photography leads and type supports —
  send to `fashion`, `beauty`, or `photography`.
- UI mockups with system type — that is `uiux`.

## Key visual axes
- **Hierarchy of glyphs**, not hierarchy of products
- **Foundry / studio editorial** look (Klim, Commercial Type,
  Jessica Hische, House Industries lineage)
- Restrained palette anchored on ink-on-paper
- Drawn or typeset letterforms treated as artwork

## Templates in domain
| id | form factor | grader | use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| typography_specimen_poster | poster 4:5 1024x1280 | text_fidelity_dense | typeface specimen plate — alphabet + glyph grid + metadata |
| typography_lettering_art   | poster 1:1 1024x1024 | text_fidelity_dense | hand-lettered single-phrase art piece for print or gallery |

## Forbidden
Real commercial typeface names (Helvetica, Gotham...), real
foundry logos, photographic imagery, drop shadows, neon glow.
