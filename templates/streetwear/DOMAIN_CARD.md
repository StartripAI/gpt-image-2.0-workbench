# streetwear — Domain Card

## Lens
Youth-culture apparel and sneaker drop-press work — capsule lookbook
plates and sneaker hero shots. Drop calendar, hype aesthetic, and
urban context anchor the visual grammar. The garment-or-sneaker
silhouette is the subject; the figure (when present) carries it.

## FOR
- Drop-announcement lookbook plates with date and item credits
- Single sneaker hero shots in stage-lit isolation
- Hype-aesthetic capsule press artifacts for indie labels

## NOT FOR
- Editorial runway or fashion-week show artifacts (use `fashion`)
- Brand campaign hero key visuals across an OOH placement plan
  (use `advertising`)
- Generic apparel flatlays or styling boards (use `fashion_flatlay_board`)
- E-commerce product cards and feature callouts (use `product` /
  `ecommerce`)

## Key visual axes
- Drop-culture zine print vs. editorial-runway gloss
- Sneaker isolation honesty vs. retail-loud photography
- Concrete-and-ink palette discipline with a single charged accent

## Forbidden
Real famous streetwear / sneaker brand logos, copyrighted swoosh /
stripe / trefoil marks, real-celebrity collabs, real model likenesses,
glamour-retouched plasticity.
