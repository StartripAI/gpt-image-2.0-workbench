# Domain: isometric_illustration

Strict 30-degree / 30-degree axonometric projection — city blocks,
workspaces, exploded views. Plan-readable, never cinematic.

## FOR
- True-isometric city blocks with named buildings + leader-line labels
- Workspace / studio scenes broken into labeled stations
- Plan-axonometric explainer art for onboarding and codex decks
- Flat-shaded vector-style aesthetics over 3D-CG

## NOT FOR
- 3D-CG photoreal renders (use `gaming/` for game-art renders)
- Flat 2D infographics with no depth (use `infographic_data/`)
- Architecture facade elevations (use `architecture/`)
- Cinematic perspective scenes (use `gaming/` or `science_fiction_concept/`)
- Sequential storytelling (use `comic_book/` or `anime/`)

## Key visual axes
- 30-30 axonometric grid — no two-point perspective, no recession
- Two-tone flat shading per surface (base + one shadow step)
- Crisp 1-pixel silhouette outlines, color-block interior planes
- Leader-line labels sit beside (not on top of) named geometry
- Restrained palette + a single accent color for rhythm

## Templates in domain
- `isometric_city_block` — infographic, 1:1, named block + buildings
- `isometric_workspace_scene` — infographic, 16:9, three labeled stations
