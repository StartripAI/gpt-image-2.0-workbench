# Domain: watercolor_illustration

Water-bound pigment medium — transparent washes on visibly textured
cotton-rag paper. Soft edges, granulation, paper grain everywhere.

## FOR
- Botanical specimen plates with latin captions
- Three-quarter character portraits in transparent washes
- Hand-painted editorial illustration with painterly imperfection
- Domains where paper grain and pigment behavior are part of the meaning

## NOT FOR
- Vector or digital flat color (use `uiux/` or `infographic_data/`)
- Photoreal portraits or product shots (use `photography/`)
- Inked anime / manga line art (use `anime/`)
- Scientific pen-and-ink diagrams (use `academic/`)
- Comic-book halftone (use `comic_book/`)

## Key visual axes
- Wet-into-wet vs dry-brush — the painter's two main verbs
- Visible cold-press paper grain across the whole frame
- Desaturated, atmospheric palettes (never neon)
- Granulating pigment, deliberate water-tide marks tolerated
- Generous bare-paper margins; type set small and quiet

## Templates in domain
- `watercolor_botanical_study` — poster, 4:5, single specimen + latin caption
- `watercolor_character_portrait` — poster, 4:5, three-quarter figure in washes
