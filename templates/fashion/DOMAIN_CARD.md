# fashion — Domain Card

## Lens
Apparel-centric editorial work — styling, fabric, silhouette. The
garment is the subject; the figure merely carries it.

## Templates
| id | form factor | grader | use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| fashion_lookbook_page | poster 4:5 1024x1280 | text_fidelity_dense | single-look hero plate with brand caption and item credits |
| fashion_flatlay_board | infographic 1:1 1024x1024 | layout | top-down styled arrangement of garments and accessories |
| fashion_runway_poster | poster 9:16 1088x1920 | text_fidelity_dense | brutalist fashion-week show announcement; vertical print |

## Choose fashion over its siblings when
- The work is about **the garment** — fabric drape, styling, the
  collection. Pick fashion.
- The brief is a generic editorial portrait with no apparel emphasis.
  Use `photography_editorial_portrait`.
- The brief is a paid ad campaign promoting a fashion product. Send to
  `advertising`.
- A packaged product wrapper / carton. Send to `product` or `food`.

## Bilingual notes
All three render zh-CN + en. Brand names, look numbers, season tags,
city-and-date plates, and item lists are typeset directly — graders
expect dense, legible typography.

## Forbidden
Real famous brands, copyrighted house monograms, real-celebrity
likenesses, photographic franchise IP, HDR oversaturation.
