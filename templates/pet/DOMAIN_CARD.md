# pet — Domain Card

## Lens
Companion-animal-centric design artifacts — adoption flyers, pet-food
packaging, branded pet-portrait plates. The animal is the emotional
anchor of a designed object, not just a photographic subject.

## FOR
- Single-animal adoption flyers for shelters and foster networks
- Pet-food bag and can labels with feeding metadata
- Branded pet-care print artifacts that center on the animal

## NOT FOR
- Human food packaging or recipe artifacts (use `food`)
- Generic photographic pet portraits with no design framing
  (use `photography_editorial_portrait`)
- Pet products as commodity SKUs without animal-centric framing
  (use `product_packaging_concept`)
- Veterinary clinical or dashboard work (use `infographic_data`)

## Key visual axes
- Animal as emotional anchor vs. animal as photographic subject
- Warm community-print honesty vs. corporate slickness
- Companion-trust aesthetic (calm pose, soft daylight, lived-in)

## Forbidden
Real shelter-chain logos, real famous pet-food brands, real-celebrity
pet likenesses, kennel-bar imagery, clinical-fluorescent grading.
