# science_fiction_concept — Domain Card

## Lens
Painterly sci-fi concept art for pre-production — keyframes, vehicle
hero plates, alien-environment matte paintings. Silhouette-first
design discipline; the world is implied, not photographed.

## FOR
- Cinematic sci-fi keyframes / matte paintings of alien landscapes or
  far-future urban scenes.
- Hero side-view plates of fictional spacecraft, mechs, ground rigs.
- Pitch-deck and worldbuilding visuals for fictional IP.

## NOT FOR
- In-game HUD overlays or item cards — use `gaming/`.
- Photoreal photography of real or pretended-real scenes — use
  `photography/`.
- Realistic architecture of real or styled buildings — use
  `architecture/`.
- Sequential paneled storytelling — use `comic_book/` or `anime/`.

## Key axes
- Painterly, not 3D-CG plastic; matte paint over chrome render.
- Silhouette readability before surface detail.
- Aerial perspective and key-light discipline carry depth.
- Always fictional — no real-IP ships, real city skylines, or
  licensed franchise insignia.

## Templates in domain
- `science_fiction_concept_keyframe` — poster, 16:9 (1920x1088),
  `text_fidelity_sparse`. Cinematic alien-landscape / far-future-urban
  matte painting; tiny footer scene-label only.
- `science_fiction_concept_vehicle_hero` — poster, 16:9 (1920x1088),
  `text_fidelity_sparse`. Orthographic side-view hero plate of a
  fictional vehicle on a clean concept plate.

## Forbidden
Real-IP spaceships, real military insignia, real city skylines, real
public figures, photoreal HDR gloss, plastic 3D-CG finish, on-screen
HUD overlays, lens flare, rainbow gradients.
