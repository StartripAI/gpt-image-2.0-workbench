# ecommerce — domain card

**This domain is FOR**
- D2C product detail pages and marketplace search-grid tiles
- Sales-focused, conversion-driven imagery (price + CTA + badge)
- Storefront category banners that tease seasonal collections

**This domain is NOT for**
- Industrial machine documentation, exploded views, or factory signage (use `industrial`)
- Design-press packaging hero shots or feature callouts (use `product`)
- Fashion editorial or lookbook imagery (separate vertical, not yet authored)
- Lifestyle / in-use photography of human models with the product

**Key visual axes (what differentiates the three templates here)**
1. **Background isolation level** — flat brand-color (hero) vs warm off-white (marketplace) vs graduated palette wash (banner)
2. **Aspect & grid context** — 4:5 portrait detail-page hero vs 1:1 search-grid tile vs 16:9 landing banner
3. **Conversion-element prominence** — full CTA button (hero) vs single trust badge (marketplace) vs CTA + sub-text (banner)

**Templates in this domain**
- `ecommerce_product_hero` — single-SKU 4:5 detail-page hero with price chip and primary CTA
- `ecommerce_marketplace_card` — compact 1:1 search-grid tile with vendor, price, single trust badge
- `ecommerce_category_banner` — wide 16:9 storefront banner introducing a seasonal category collection
