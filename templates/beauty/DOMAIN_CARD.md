# beauty — Domain Card

## FOR
Cosmetics and skincare work specifically: bottles, jars, droppers,
swatches, and clean-lit beauty editorial close-ups. The product or
the cared-for skin is the subject.

## NOT FOR
- Garment-led editorial — that is `fashion` (lookbooks, runway).
- Edible / drinkable products — that is `food` (packaging,
  styling).
- Generic non-beauty product packaging (electronics, hardware) —
  that is `product`.
- Beauty hero ads with strong brand-name copy — that is
  `advertising`.

## Key visual axes
- **Object-and-skin only**, never staged in a busy retail context
- **Clean-beauty / quiet-luxury** finish (Aesop / Byredo /
  Glossier press lineage, no copying)
- Honest skin texture, no plastic smoothing
- Tonal palettes keyed to a single mood word

## Forbidden
Real-celebrity likeness, real famous beauty brand logos, HDR
plasticity, heavy makeup mask, rainbow palettes.
