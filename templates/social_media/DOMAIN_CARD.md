# Domain: social_media

Platform-native feed creative — designed for thumb-scroll attention,
not for ad media buys or general web UI.

## FOR
- 1:1 launch announcement posts for Instagram or X feeds
- 9:16 vertical 3-frame story arcs for Instagram or TikTok stories
- 1:1 YouTube-style thumbnail review boards comparing 3 candidates
- Bold flat-color, high-contrast feed-friendly composition

## NOT FOR
- Generic web product UI mockups (use `uiux/`)
- Brand campaign hero or OOH ads (use `advertising/`)
- Xiaohongshu-specific cover layouts already in
  `uiux/social_cover_xhs`
- E-commerce listing photos or product SKUs (use `ecommerce/`)

## Key visual axes
- Thumbnail-scale legibility (works at 100px tall)
- Feed-attention hierarchy (headline > date/source > CTA)
- Story-arc continuity across 3 frames
- High-contrast color blocking with disciplined type

## Templates in domain
- `social_media_launch_post` — poster, 1:1, IG/X launch announcement
- `social_media_story_sequence` — poster, 9:16, 3-frame story arc
- `social_media_thumbnail_grid` — poster, 1:1, 3-up YT thumbnail board
