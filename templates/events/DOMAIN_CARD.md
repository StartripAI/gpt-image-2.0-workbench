# events — Domain Card

## FOR
Date-and-time-anchored announcements: concerts, festivals,
weddings, gallery openings, talks, gigs. The poster has a
specific WHEN and WHERE that anchor the reading.

## NOT FOR
- Generic brand hero key visuals (no specific date) — those go
  to `advertising`.
- Social-media tile-and-caption posts — that is `social_media`.
- Magazine editorial spreads — those go to `fashion`, `beauty`,
  or `photography`.
- Type-as-art lettering pieces with no event payload — that is
  `typography`.

## Key visual axes
- **Date and venue** as required reading hierarchy
- Independent gig-poster lineage for music; fine-press lineage
  for weddings and formal announcements
- Print-ready palettes (limited screen-print or letterpress feel)
- No identifying portrait of any real performer or couple

## Templates in domain
| id | form factor | grader | use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| events_concert_poster | poster 9:16 1088x1920 | text_fidelity_dense | gig / festival announcement, headliner + support + date + venue |
| events_wedding_invite | poster 4:5 1024x1280 | text_fidelity_dense | formal wedding invitation card with names, date, venue, RSVP |

## Forbidden
Real-celebrity likeness, real famous band / venue / festival
logos, glitter or gold-chrome gradients, rainbow palettes.
