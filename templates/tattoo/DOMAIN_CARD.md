# tattoo — Domain Card

## FOR
Tattoo art shown on isolated paper or stencil substrate — flash
sheets and single-design plates. The artwork is the inked design
itself, ready to be transferred.

## NOT FOR
- Body or skin photography (tattooed person posed) — that is
  `photography`.
- General illustration in watercolor or pen-and-ink — those are
  `watercolor_illustration` or `comic_book`.
- Comic-page sequential art — that is `comic_book`.
- Type-as-art lettering pieces with no tattoo motif — that is
  `typography`.

## Key visual axes
- **Black ink dominance**, optional limited spot color
- Traditional / dotwork / fine-line / minimal lineages
- Substrate is paper, not skin — no photographic body context
- Design reads as transferable line art, not painterly art

| id | form factor | grader | use when |

## Forbidden
Photographic skin or body, real famous artist signatures, real
famous shop logos, neon glow, 3D rendering, rainbow palettes.
