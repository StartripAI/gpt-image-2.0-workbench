# industrial — domain card

**This domain is FOR**
- Machinery, engineering, manufacturing, and plant-floor imagery
- Technically accurate diagrams (process flows, exploded cutaways) for service manuals and operator training
- Line-side safety / hazard / PPE notices

**This domain is NOT for**
- Ecommerce shop tiles or marketplace product imagery (use `ecommerce`)
- Branded packaging hero shots or product feature callouts (use `product`)
- Architectural facades or building exteriors (use `architecture`)
- Lifestyle photography or human-operator portraits

**Key visual axes (what differentiates the three templates here)**
1. **Information density** — multi-step horizontal flow (process diagram) vs single-machine annotated view (cutaway) vs hazard-first emergency message (safety poster)
2. **Color discipline** — slate-grey + safety-orange (process) vs steel + signal red/blue (cutaway) vs strict OSHA yellow/black/red (safety)
3. **Rendering register** — flat schematic glyphs (process) vs technical-illustration linework (cutaway) vs sign-grade pictograms (safety)

**Templates in this domain**
- `industrial_process_diagram` — 16:9 four-stage process-flow diagram with arrows and step labels
- `industrial_cutaway_view` — 3:2 isometric cutaway of a machine with four numbered component callouts
- `industrial_safety_poster` — 9:16 line-side safety / hazard / PPE notice in OSHA-style register
